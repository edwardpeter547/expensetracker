# Target Users and Personas

**Status:** Proposal. Personas are synthesized from product strategy and competitor patterns, not from conducted user interviews.

## Primary segment
**Privacy-conscious individuals and households in the UK/EU** who want control over spending and cash flow without connecting every bank account on day one. Secondary segment: side-income earners with irregular income.

## Personas

### 1. Manual Tracker (primary free user)
- **Profile:** 25–40, employed, currently uses a spreadsheet or notes app.
- **Jobs to be done:** record spending quickly, see monthly totals, avoid overspending.
- **Needs:** fast capture, simple categories, a monthly dashboard, CSV export.
- **Frustrations:** setup fatigue, bank-linking fear, ads, unclear pricing.
- **Upgrade trigger:** wants budgets, recurring bills, and searchable history.

### 2. Budget Improver
- **Profile:** 28–45, has tried a budgeting app and lapsed.
- **Jobs to be done:** keep budgets realistic, carry over overspending, get alerts.
- **Needs:** rollover budgets, flexible periods, budget-vs-actual clarity.
- **Frustrations:** rigid methods and all-or-nothing discipline.
- **Upgrade trigger:** rollover budgets and alerts (Plus).

### 3. Irregular Earner
- **Profile:** freelancer, gig worker, or seasonal earner.
- **Jobs to be done:** know whether funds cover upcoming bills before payday.
- **Needs:** recurring transaction modeling and cash-flow forecasting.
- **Frustrations:** fixed monthly budgets that do not fit variable income.
- **Upgrade trigger:** forecasting and scenario tools (Pro).

### 4. Household Lead
- **Profile:** 30–50, manages shared household money with a partner.
- **Jobs to be done:** share budgets and goals while keeping some personal money private.
- **Needs:** shared and personal spaces, roles, settlement tracking.
- **Frustrations:** shared apps that collapse everything into one view.
- **Upgrade trigger:** household collaboration (Family add-on or tier).

### 5. Privacy-Conscious Saver
- **Profile:** 25–55, distrusts ad-funded finance apps.
- **Jobs to be done:** retain control of financial data and understand what happens to it.
- **Needs:** clear privacy posture, export, deletion, no data selling.
- **Frustrations:** opaque data use and hard-to-find export/delete controls.
- **Upgrade trigger:** trusts the product enough to pay for advanced features.

## Segment not targeted initially
- Enterprises and employee expense reimbursement (defer Business tier).
- Investors needing brokerage portfolio tracking (defer until core value is proven).
